How to use this

Type the two arb sides and their decimal prices once. All three tabs use them.

  1. COVER BOTH — before anything is placed. Splits the budget so both sides return the same, then rounds each stake up.
  2. ONE ALREADY PLACED — a bet is on and the other side has moved. Put in the stake you placed and the prices as they are now; it sizes the other bet to break even, or to a target profit, if that side wins.
  3. ARB + DOUBLE — the arb plus the same second selection on both sides as doubles. OPTIMISE STAKES puts the least it can on the singles to keep your loss inside the limit and the rest on the doubles. Or use the slider, or type the four stakes yourself.

A double is not a hedge. If the second selection loses, both doubles are dead and only one single has paid, so the position is usually down. That loss is the number you choose with “risk at most”, and this page always shows it plainly.

Stakes round up to whole pounds, so the bets usually come to a little over your budget. Every outcome is worked out from the stakes as listed, so the numbers reconcile if you check them.
