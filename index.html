How to use this
  1. Put the two arb prices in, and the price of the extra selection you want to add to both sides.
  2. Set your budget, then press OPTIMISE STAKES. It puts the least it can on the singles — just enough to keep your loss inside the limit — and everything else on the doubles, which is what makes the most when the second selection wins.
  3. Or move the slider yourself. More on doubles means more profit if the second selection wins and a bigger loss if it does not.
  4. Place the four bets exactly as listed. The doubles are your side of the arb and the second selection on one slip.

A double is not a hedge. If the second selection loses, both doubles are dead and only one single has paid, so the position is usually down. That loss is the number you choose with “risk at most”. This page always shows it plainly rather than calling the position covered.

Stakes round up to whole pounds, so four bets usually come to a little over your budget. Everything below is worked out from the stakes as listed, so the numbers reconcile if you check them.
